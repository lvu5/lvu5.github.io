I'm a Research Associate II at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in Abu Dhabi, where I work on zero-shot multilabel text classification.

I earned my B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago. There I did research on algorithmic fairness, which led to Superhuman Fairness at ICML 2023. I've also taught Python and operating systems at National Louis University, mentored master's projects, and built full-stack tools for research and journalism.

Research interestsNatural language processing, especially how misinformation and propaganda spread on social media.
